# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We want participation in Knowledge Graph Editor to be welcoming, constructive, and safe for everyone, regardless of background or level of experience.

## Expected behavior

Contributors and maintainers should:

- communicate respectfully and focus criticism on ideas and code, not people;
- assume good intent while remaining clear about technical or community concerns;
- welcome questions and differing levels of experience;
- give credit for ideas, code, testing, documentation, and other contributions;
- avoid harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized conduct, or sustained disruption;
- respect privacy and do not publish another person's private information without permission.

## Maintainer responsibilities

Maintainers may edit, reject, hide, or remove comments, commits, code, issues, or other contributions that violate this Code of Conduct. Repeated or serious violations may result in temporary or permanent restrictions from project spaces.

## Reporting

If a conduct issue cannot be handled comfortably in public, contact the maintainers through a private channel available on the repository. Reports should be handled as confidentially as reasonably possible.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in project-controlled spaces and when someone is officially representing the project elsewhere.
